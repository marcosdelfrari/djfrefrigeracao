Use this skill when a user wants to:

- Schedule a technician visit
- Get a repair or maintenance quote
- Send a photo/video for a quick pre-assessment

## What to collect before linking

Ask briefly for:

1. Equipment type (geladeira, ar-condicionado, freezer, expositor, cervejeira, frigobar)
2. Brand and brief symptom
3. Neighborhood / city in the BH metro area
4. Whether they can send a photo or short video

Then open the WhatsApp URL with a concise Portuguese summary.
